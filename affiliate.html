MANDATORY STATUTORY STATEMENT

“As an Amazon Associate, 5minSummary (5minssummary.com) earns from qualifying purchases. We also participate in selective affiliate programs for books, educational resources, web hosting, and productivity tools.”

1. What Is an Affiliate Link and How Does It Work?

Running a fast, research-driven publication and developing client-side web utilities (such as our 8K Photo Enhancer, financial calculators, and converters) requires ongoing engineering time, domain infrastructure, and editorial research. To keep 5minSummary accessible to every student and reader without locking articles behind subscription paywalls, some of the links on our website are affiliate referral links.

When you click on a special link pointing to an online retailer or service provider (such as Amazon, Hostinger, or a book retailer) and complete a qualifying purchase within the merchant's standard referral window, that merchant pays us a small referral commission out of their own marketing budget.

Does Clicking an Affiliate Link Cost You Extra?

Never. Whether you purchase an item through our referral link or open a new tab and type the store's URL directly, the price you pay at checkout is 100% identical:

2. Amazon Associates Program Disclosure

5minSummary (5minssummary.com) is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program and the Amazon India Associates Program, which are affiliate advertising programs designed to provide a means for websites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.in, Amazon.com, and affiliated international Amazon marketplaces.

When we summarize non-fiction books (such as Atomic Habits, The Psychology of Money, Deep Work, or The Lean Startup) or recommend study accessories, hardware, and everyday utilities, we often include a direct link to the book or product on Amazon so readers can easily explore the full edition or verify verified-buyer specifications.

3. Our Editorial Independence & Review Ethics

In accordance with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) 16 CFR Part 255 Guidelines and the Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) Digital Influencer & Publisher Guidelines, we enforce a strict firewall between our editorial content and affiliate monetization:

  • No Paid Positive Ratings: We do not accept cash bribes or hidden payments from manufacturers to write artificially positive summaries or product evaluations.
  • Honest Pros & Cons: Whenever a product, tool, or hosting service has limitations, we state them clearly so you can make an informed decision before spending your money.
  • Non-Affiliate Links Included: If the best resource, open-source software, or reference book for a topic does not offer an affiliate program, we link to it anyway without hesitation.

4. Frequently Asked Reader Questions

Can 5minSummary see my personal shopping details or address? +

No. All transactions take place directly on the secure servers of the merchant (e.g., Amazon). We never have access to your name, email address, home address, or payment method. We only see aggregate report numbers showing that an item was ordered through our referral tag.

What happens if I return a product bought through an affiliate link? +

Your return, warranty, and refund rights with the retailer remain 100% unchanged. If you return an item, you receive your full refund from the retailer as normal, and any referral commission is automatically reversed.
