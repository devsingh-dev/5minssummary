OUR STORY

Less scrolling.
More understanding.

How a simple idea—learning one worthwhile concept in five minutes—became an independent space for curious minds.

The modern internet gives us infinite information, yet very little clarity. Every day, millions of people want to read great books, understand how ancient civilizations rose and fell, grasp the wonders of science, and stay informed on global affairs—but find themselves trapped in endless noisy feeds.

"You don't need hours of free time to grow wiser. You just need five focused minutes every day."

Why We Built 5minSummary

5minSummary was created with a single mission: to respect your time and your curiosity. Whether it is distilling a 300-page non-fiction book into ten actionable takeaways (in both English and Hindi), explaining quantum physics in plain language, or offering a calm weekly roundup of current affairs, everything we publish is designed to be completed in five minutes.
