EDITORIAL APPROACH

How we distill big ideas into five minutes.

Our framework for selecting books, verifying historical & scientific context, and writing without fluff.

PRINCIPLE 01

Signal Over Sensationalism

Whether we are summarizing weekly current affairs or explaining a scientific phenomenon, we strip away clickbait and emotional noise. We focus on the underlying mechanisms—why something happened, how it works, and why it matters in the long run.

PRINCIPLE 02

Structured 10-Point Book Briefings

Every book in our library goes through a two-part synthesis: a cohesive 5-minute narrative summary capturing the author's core thesis, paired with 10 structured key takeaways so you can immediately apply the book's mental models in daily life.

PRINCIPLE 03

Accessible in English & Hindi

Great ideas belong to everyone. Our book summaries are crafted in both clear English and natural Hindi so readers can explore complex concepts in the language they think best in.

PRINCIPLE 04

Respect for the Reader's Attention

Our dedicated article reader removes pop-ups, auto-playing videos, and visual clutter. Every piece is timed to take between 3 to 5 minutes—complete with keyboard navigation (Backspace) so you stay in control.
