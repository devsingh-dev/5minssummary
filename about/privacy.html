PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy, explained simply.

Effective Date: October 2026 • How 5minSummary handles data, cookies, and browser storage.

1. Information We Collect

You can browse 5minSummary, read book summaries, explore history and science explainers, and use our interactive calculators and games without creating an account. We only collect personal information (such as your email address or name) when you voluntarily subscribe to our newsletter or submit our contact form.

2. Local Browser Storage (Bookmarks & Tools)

When you save or bookmark books and articles, or use our interactive utilities and games, your preferences are stored locally in your own browser using localStorage. This data stays on your device and can be cleared anytime by clearing your browser cache.

3. Analytics & Cookies

We use standard analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) and cookies to understand aggregate traffic patterns—such as which summaries or tools are most helpful to readers—and to serve relevant advertisements via third-party partners like Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this or other websites.

4. External & Affiliate Links

Our book summary pages include links to purchase full books on external platforms such as Amazon and Flipkart. Once you click an external link, you are governed by the privacy policy of that respective platform.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us via our Contact page.
