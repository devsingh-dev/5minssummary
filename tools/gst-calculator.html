HOW TO USE THIS CALCULATOR

Adding or Extracting GST for Invoices

  1. Choose Exclusive or Inclusive Mode: Click + Add GST (Exclusive) if you have a base price and want to add tax on top, or click − Remove GST (Inclusive) to reverse-calculate the base price from an MRP or gross bill.
  2. Pick an Official GST Slab: Select 3% (Gold/Jewelry), 5% (Essentials), 12% (Processed Goods), 18% (IT Services & Electronics), or 28% (Luxury items).
  3. Switch Between CGST/SGST and IGST: Select Intra-State for transactions within the same state (50/50 split between Central and State GST) or Inter-State for cross-state IGST billing.

TAXATION FORMULAS

How We Calculate Exclusive & Inclusive GST

1. Adding GST (Exclusive): $$\text{GST Amount} = \frac{\text{Net Price} \times R}{100}, \quad \text{Gross} = \text{Net} + \text{GST}$$

2. Removing GST (Inclusive Reverse Calculation): $$\text{Net Price} = \frac{\text{Gross Price} \times 100}{100 + R}, \quad \text{GST} = \text{Gross} - \text{Net}$$
