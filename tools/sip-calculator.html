HOW TO USE THIS CALCULATOR

Planning Long-Term Mutual Fund Investments

  1. Set Monthly Investment: Choose how much you plan to invest every month (e.g., ₹5,000 or ₹10,000).
  2. Expected Return Rate: Equity mutual funds historically target 12%–15% annualized returns over long horizons.
  3. Enable Step-Up: If your salary increases yearly, use the annual step-up slider to automatically scale your monthly SIP.

COMPOUND INTEREST FORMULA

How SIP Returns Are Calculated

The future value of a Systematic Investment Plan is computed using the annuity formula: $$\text{FV} = P \times \left( \frac{(1 + i)^n - 1}{i} \right) \times (1 + i)$$

Where $P$ is monthly investment, $i$ is monthly rate of return ($\text{Annual Rate} / 12 / 100$), and $n$ is total months.
