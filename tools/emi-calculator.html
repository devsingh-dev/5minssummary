MATHEMATICAL FORMULA

How We Calculate Monthly EMI

Banks calculate Equated Monthly Installments (EMI) on a monthly reducing-balance basis using the standard compound formula: $$\text{EMI} = \frac{P \times r \times (1 + r)^n}{(1 + r)^n - 1}$$

Where $P$ is the Principal loan amount, $r = \frac{\text{Annual Rate}}{12 \times 100}$ is the monthly interest rate, and $n$ is the total number of monthly installments ($\text{Years} \times 12$).
