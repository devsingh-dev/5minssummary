HOW TO USE THIS VIDEO TOOL

Trimming Clips & Capturing Still Frames

  1. Load Your Video: Select any MP4, WebM, or MOV video file from your device. It loads immediately without uploading to the internet.
  2. Set Start & End Points: Drag the Start Time and End Time sliders to isolate the exact highlight segment you want to keep.
  3. Adjust Visual Look: Fine-tune Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, or convert the clip to classic Black & White.
  4. Save Snapshot or Video Clip: Click 📸 Capture Current Frame (.PNG) to save a full-resolution still photo of the current scene, or click 🎬 Export Trimmed Video Clip (.WebM) to record and download your cut segment.

LOCAL MEDIA PROCESSING

How Browser Video Exporting Works

Your video file stays entirely on your computer or phone. When you capture a frame or export a trimmed clip, the tool draws each video frame onto an internal rendering surface with your color adjustments applied.

For video clip exports, the browser's built-in MediaRecorder captures the real-time playback between your start and end timestamps and packages it into a clean, downloadable .WebM video file.
