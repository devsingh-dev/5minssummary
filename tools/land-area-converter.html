HOW TO USE THIS CONVERTER

Converting Agricultural & Residential Land Units

  1. Select Your Regional Bigha Standard: While units like Acre, Hectare, Dismil, and Gaj are fixed nationwide, Bigha varies by state. Pick your state region at the top-right for accurate local revenue conversions.
  2. Enter Value & Pick Units: Type your land area and select any source and destination unit (e.g., Hectare to Acre, Dismil to Square Feet, or Square Feet to Gaj).
  3. Use the Plot Calculator: Measuring a residential plot? Enter the length and width in feet at the bottom-left and click Send to Converter.

STANDARD CONVERSION FACTORS

How We Calculate Land Area

All conversions are anchored to exact Square Feet (sq.ft) equivalents before converting to the target unit:

  • 1 Acre = 43,560 sq.ft = 100 Dismil
  • 1 Hectare = 107,639.10 sq.ft = 2.47105 Acres
  • 1 Gaj (Sq. Yard) = 9 sq.ft
  • 1 Dismil (Decimal) = 435.6 sq.ft
  • 1 Guntha = 1,089 sq.ft (40 Guntha = 1 Acre)
