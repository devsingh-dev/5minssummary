HOW TO USE THIS CALCULATOR

Mastering Basic & Scientific Operations

  • Standard Arithmetic & Parentheses: Type expressions directly using your keyboard or on-screen buttons, such as (25 + 15) * 4 / 2, and press Enter or =.
  • Angle Mode (DEG vs RAD): Switch between DEG (Degrees) and RAD (Radians) at the top-left before computing trigonometric functions ($\sin, \cos, \tan$) or inverse functions ($\sin^{-1}, \cos^{-1}, \tan^{-1}$).
  • Exponents & Roots: Use $x^y$ to raise any number to a custom power (e.g., 2^10 = 1024) or click $\sqrt{x}$, $x^2$, and $x^3$ for instant powers.
  • Memory & History Tape: Store running totals with M+ and M−, recall them with MR, or click any previous calculation in the right-hand History Tape to load it back onto the screen.

MATHEMATICAL PRECISION

How We Calculate Expressions

Our engine strictly follows the BODMAS / PEMDAS order of operations: Parentheses first, followed by Exponents ($x^y$), Multiplication and Division, and finally Addition and Subtraction.

To prevent standard IEEE-754 floating-point rounding artifacts (where 0.1 + 0.2 would otherwise display 0.30000000000000004), results are automatically normalized to 12 significant decimal places.
