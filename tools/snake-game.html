RETRO ARCADE

Snake Game

Use Arrow Keys or WASD to navigate. Eat red apples to grow longer and score points.

HOW TO PLAY

Rules & Keyboard Controls

  1. Navigation: Use your keyboard's Arrow Keys or W, A, S, D to steer the snake across the grid.
  2. Eat Pellets: Every red apple you consume increases your score by 1 point and lengthens your tail by one segment.
  3. Avoid Crashes: The game ends if the snake hits the outer boundary wall or runs directly into its own body.

GRID ENGINE

How Movement & Collision Work

The game board is divided into a $20 \times 20$ coordinate tile grid ($20\text{px}$ per tile). On every game tick, the snake's head coordinates update based on the current velocity vector, and tail segments follow forward.
