HOW TO USE THIS TRACKER

Checking Local & Global Weather Conditions

  1. Search Any City or Town: Type the name of your city, district, or international destination in the search bar and click Search Weather.
  2. One-Click GPS Detection: Click 📍 My Location to let your browser fetch hyper-local meteorological data for your exact latitude and longitude.

METEOROLOGICAL DATA

How We Calculate Weather Metrics

Live atmospheric observations are powered by the open-source Open-Meteo global numerical weather prediction models, combining national weather service radar and satellite feeds.

Apparent ("Feels Like") Temperature is computed using the Steadman heat-index and wind-chill model, factoring in air temperature, relative humidity vapor pressure, and wind speed at $10\text{ meters}$ elevation.
