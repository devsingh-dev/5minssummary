HOW TO PLAY

Building Sky-High Towers Without Losing Width

  1. Time Your Drop: Watch the horizontal sliding block and tap or press Spacebar the exact moment it aligns with the block below it.
  2. Watch Out for Slicing: Any part of the block that hangs over the left or right edge is sliced away, making the next floor narrower and harder to hit.
  3. Trigger Perfect Combos: Dropping a block within $\pm 4\text{ pixels}$ of dead-center snaps it to 100% alignment and awards a 🔥 Perfect Streak bonus!

INTERVAL OVERLAP MATH

How Block Slicing Is Calculated

Given the top resting block interval $[x_1, x_1 + w_1]$ and the newly dropped block $[x_2, x_2 + w_2]$, the surviving intersection width $W_{\text{new}}$ is computed as: $$W_{\text{new}} = \min(x_1 + w_1, x_2 + w_2) - \max(x_1, x_2)$$

If $W_{\text{new}} \le 0$, the dropped block missed the tower completely and the round ends.
