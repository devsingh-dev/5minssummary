HOW TO PLAY TETRIS

Mastering Tetromino Stacking & Line Clears

  1. Align & Rotate Shapes: Guide the 7 geometric Tetrominoes (I, O, T, S, Z, J, L) left or right and rotate them so they interlock without leaving empty gaps.
  2. Use the Ghost Piece: A translucent outline at the bottom of the matrix shows the exact landing spot if you press Spacebar for an instant Hard Drop.
  3. Save Pieces in the Hold Slot: Press C (or click Swap) to store an inconvenient piece in the Hold box and swap it back when needed.

SCORING FORMULA

How Line Multipliers & Levels Work

Points scale with your current Level ($L$) and reward multi-line clears exponentially:

  • 1 Line (Single): 100 × L pts
  • 2 Lines (Double): 300 × L pts
  • 3 Lines (Triple): 500 × L pts
  • 4 Lines (Tetris!): 800 × L pts

Every 10 lines cleared advances you to the next Level, reducing the gravity drop interval for a faster challenge.
