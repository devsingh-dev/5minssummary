CALENDAR ARITHMETIC

How We Calculate Exact Calendar Age

Unlike simple calculators that divide total days by $365.25$, our chronological engine performs exact Gregorian calendar borrowing across variable month lengths ($28, 29, 30, \text{ or } 31\text{ days}$).

Leap years are evaluated using the astronomical rule: a year is a leap year if it is divisible by $4$, except century years unless divisible by $400$.
