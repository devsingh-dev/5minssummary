HOW TO PLAY

Rules & Winning Forks

  1. Align Your Symbols: Players take turns placing X and O on the grid. Connect 3 in a row (on the 3×3 board) or 4 in a row (on the 4×4 board) horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.
  2. Create a Fork: Against human opponents or Casual AI, aim to control the center or three corners to create two simultaneous winning threats that cannot both be blocked.

COMBINATORIAL GAME THEORY

How the Unbeatable Minimax AI Works

Tic-Tac-Toe is a solved zero-sum game with $255,168$ possible 3×3 game trajectories. In Unbeatable AI mode, our engine runs the recursive Minimax algorithm: $$V(s) = \max_{a} \min_{b} \text{Utility}(s')$$

The AI simulates every possible future board state to the end of the match, assigning $+10 - \text{depth}$ to AI victories, $-10 + \text{depth}$ to human victories, and $0$ to draws—guaranteeing that the AI will never lose.
