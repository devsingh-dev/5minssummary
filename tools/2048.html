STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

How to Play 2048

2048 is a single-player mathematical sliding block puzzle played on a 4×4 grid. The objective is to slide numbered tiles across the board to combine them and create a tile with the number 2048.

  1. Slide the Tiles: Use your keyboard's Arrow keys (or W, A, S, D) on desktop, or simply swipe in any direction (Up, Down, Left, Right) on mobile.Every tile on the board will slide as far as possible in that chosen direction.
  2. Merge Matching Numbers: When two tiles carrying the exact same number collide while moving, they merge into a single tile equal to their sum (for example, two 2s become 4, two 4s become 8, and two 1024s become 2048).
  3. Plan for New Spawns: After every valid move, a new tile (either a 2 or a 4) randomly appears in an empty spot on the board.
  4. Use Undo Strategically: Made an accidental slide? Click the ↶ Undo button at the top to revert your last move and rethink your strategy.

GAME MECHANICS & MATH

How We Calculate Your Score

Every time two tiles merge, your score increases by the value of the newly created tile. For example, combining two 64 tiles adds +128 points to your total score.

New tiles spawn with a 90% probability of being a 2 and a 10% probability of being a 4. Because tiles double exponentially ($2^1, 2^2, 2^3 \dots 2^{11} = 2048$), reaching the 2048 tile typically yields a total score between 20,000 and 22,000 points.

PRO STRATEGY

The Corner Technique

  • Pick One Corner: Choose one corner (e.g., bottom-right) and keep your highest-value tile locked there at all times.
  • Build a Descending Chain: Arrange the row next to your corner tile in descending order (e.g., 512 → 256 → 128 → 64).
  • Avoid Moving Away: If your highest tile is in the bottom-right corner, avoid pressing Up or Left unless strictly necessary.
