SI & IMPERIAL STANDARDS

How We Calculate Unit Conversions

Linear physical quantities are normalized through their International System of Units (SI) base anchor (e.g., Meter for length, Kilogram for mass, Pascal for pressure) before scaling to the destination unit.

Temperature Scales use exact offset transformations: $$^\circ\text{F} = \left(^\circ\text{C} \times \frac{9}{5}\right) + 32, \quad \text{K} = \,^\circ\text{C} + 273.15$$
