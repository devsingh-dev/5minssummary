FORMULAS USED

How We Calculate BMI & BMR

Body Mass Index (BMI) is calculated using the standard World Health Organization (WHO) equation: $\text{BMI} = \frac{\text{Weight (kg)}}{\text{Height (m)}^2}$.

Healthy BMI Categories: Underweight ($< 18.5$), Normal weight ($18.5 – 24.9$), Overweight ($25.0 – 29.9$), and Obesity ($\ge 30.0$). Daily calorie expenditure is derived using the clinically validated Mifflin-St Jeor Equation.
