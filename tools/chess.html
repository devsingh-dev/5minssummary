HOW TO PLAY CHESS

Rules, Piece Movement & Strategy

  • Selecting & Moving Pieces: Click any of your pieces to highlight all legal destination squares in green. Click a highlighted square to execute your move.
  • Piece Movement Summary: Pawns move forward 1 square (or 2 from their starting rank) and capture diagonally; Knights jump in an L-shape ($2 \times 1$); Bishops glide diagonally; Rooks command ranks and files; Queens combine Rook and Bishop power; Kings step 1 square in any direction.
  • Check, Checkmate & Promotion: You cannot make a move that leaves your King under attack. Any Pawn reaching the 8th rank automatically promotes to a Queen.

CHESS MATH & AI

How Material & AI Evaluation Work

Standard chess theory assigns relative material point values to each piece: Pawn = 1, Knight = 3, Bishop = 3, Rook = 5, and Queen = 9.

Our client-side AI evaluates candidate moves using a 2-ply lookahead search combined with center-control bonuses, king-safety checks, and material balance—giving you a responsive opponent right in your browser.
