EXCHANGE RATE METHODOLOGY

How We Calculate Cross-Currency Rates

Our converter fetches live mid-market reference rates anchored against the US Dollar (USD) and computes triangular cross-rates using the formula: $$\text{Rate}_{A \to B} = \frac{\text{Rate}_{\text{USD} \to B}}{\text{Rate}_{\text{USD} \to A}}$$

Mid-market rates represent the midpoint between global buy and sell rates on wholesale foreign exchange markets. If you are offline, the tool seamlessly falls back to cached benchmark rates so you never lose functionality.
