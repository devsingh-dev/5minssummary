HOW TO PLAY

Mastering Rhythm & Pipe Clearances

  1. Tap to Flap: Press Spacebar, click your mouse, or tap the screen to give the bird an upward velocity impulse.
  2. Stay Centered in the Gap: Look ahead at the next incoming pipe pair and aim just above the lower pipe lip so you have room to drop or flap smoothly.
  3. Unlock Arcade Medals: Score 10+ for Bronze 🥉, 25+ for Silver 🥈, 50+ for Gold 🥇, and 100+ for the Diamond Trophy 💎.

GAME ENGINE PHYSICS

How Gravity & Collision Math Work

On every animation frame ($\approx 16.6\text{ ms}$ at $60\text{ FPS}$), downward gravity acceleration $g$ is added to the bird's vertical velocity: $v_y(t+1) = v_y(t) + g$, and vertical position updates via $y(t+1) = y(t) + v_y$.

Flapping sets $v_y$ to a negative upward jump impulse ($-7.2\text{ px/frame}$). Bounding-box collision checks test whether the bird's radius intersects any upper or lower pipe rectangle.
