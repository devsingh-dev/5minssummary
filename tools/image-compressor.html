HOW TO USE THIS 2-IN-1 STUDIO

Compressing or Upscaling Photos to 8K

  1. Choose Your Mode First: At the top of the workspace, click 🗜️ Compress Size if you want to reduce KB/MB for websites and online forms, or click ✨ Enhance & 8K Upscale to boost image resolution and clarity.
  2. Upscale up to 8K Ultra HD: In Enhance mode, choose between 2× Super, 1080p FHD, 2K QHD, 4K UHD (3840px), or 8K Ultra HD (7680px). Our engine automatically preserves your photo's exact aspect ratio.
  3. Sharpen Edges & Restore Lighting: Apply a 1-click preset like Auto HD Crisp or 8K Ultra Detail, or manually adjust the Edge Sharpness convolution slider to bring out fine textures in hair, fabric, foliage, and text.
  4. Compare & Download: Slide the center comparison handle left and right to verify the improvement, then click ⬇ Download Result.

CLIENT-SIDE GPU IMAGE PROCESSING

How 8K Upscaling & Convolution Sharpening Work

Standard single-pass browser resizing often produces soft or pixelated edges when stretching a photo to 4K (3840px) or 8K (7680px). Our engine uses progressive multi-step bicubic interpolation (`imageSmoothingQuality: "high"`) combined with a hardware-accelerated **3×3 Laplacian Convolution Matrix**: $$\begin{bmatrix} 0 & -s & 0 \\ -s & 1 + 4s & -s \\ 0 & -s & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$

This mathematical kernel amplifies high-frequency luminance transitions between neighboring pixels—restoring crisp micro-contrast and edge definition right on your device's GPU without uploading your personal photos to any external server.
