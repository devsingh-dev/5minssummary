HOW TO USE THIS TOOL

Optimizing Images for Fast Websites & Forms

  1. Select Your Image: Click the upload area or drag and drop any JPG, PNG, or WebP photograph from your computer or phone.
  2. Choose Quality & Format: Set the Compression Quality slider around 75% and select WebP or JPEG to achieve dramatic file size reductions.
  3. Optional Dimension Scaling: If your camera photo is 4000px wide, slide the Resize Scale down to 50% to cut megabytes down to kilobytes instantly.
  4. Inspect & Download: Compare the visual sharpness side-by-side and click Download to save your optimized file.

COMPRESSION SCIENCE

How Browser Image Compression Works

This utility renders your uploaded image onto an off-screen HTML5 <canvas> element and encodes it locally via canvas.toBlob(callback, mimeType, quality).

Formats like JPEG and WebP use quantization and perceptual chroma subsampling—grouping similar neighboring color frequencies that the human eye barely notices—allowing an 80% reduction in bytes with virtually identical visual fidelity.
